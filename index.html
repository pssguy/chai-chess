The games

Each opens at move 1 · “Guess the move” is inside every game

Engine: Stockfish 18 (lite, single-threaded) compiled to WebAssembly, GPLv3, source at github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish and github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js. Move rules: chess.js (BSD). Pieces: Colin M.L. Burnett's set, as shipped with python-chess. Famous-game evaluations were computed in advance with Stockfish 16; your own games are analysed live in your browser.

Game score

Evaluations are Stockfish, in pawns from White's point of view (+1.00 ≈ an extra pawn for White). Move marks come from how much each move changed the engine's estimate of the winning chances: ?! inaccuracy, ? mistake, ?? blunder; ! and !! are the commentator's. Or a game link

This page can't open other websites itself, so links need one extra step: copy the game's PGN from the site and paste it above.

Where to find the PGN

Lichess: open the game, choose the Share & export (or FEN & PGN) tab under the board, and copy the PGN text.

Chess.com: open the game, press the Share button, choose the PGN tab and copy it.

Chessgames.com: use the download link on the game page, open the file and copy the text.

Clock times, comments and side-lines in the PGN are fine; they are stripped out.
